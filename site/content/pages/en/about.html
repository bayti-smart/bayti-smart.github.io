Smart Casa is an independent site that gathers well-known smart home devices sold on AliExpress, so you save research time and can compare options.

How we work

  • We pick widely available products from established stores, favouring popular items with strong buyer ratings.
  • We write product descriptions from the information published on the store listing, and we do not claim to have tested products unless we say so explicitly.
  • We do not sell products ourselves; the purchase happens on the store's website.

About the prices we show

Prices are approximate. They are the US dollar price listed on AliExpress when we added the product. Prices, shipping and availability change constantly and depend on your country, the option you choose and current promotions, so the price at checkout may be different. Always check the current price on the store page.

How the site is funded

We take part in affiliate programs, so we may earn a commission on purchases made through our links at no extra cost to you. It does not change the price you pay.

A note on compatibility

Many products are built for specific markets (mains voltage and plug shape). Read our buying guides before ordering a device for your country.
