Who we are

Smart Casa is an independent website featuring smart home product recommendations, hosted on GitHub Pages. To contact us, open an issue on the repository issues page or message us on our social profiles linked in the footer.

What data we collect

The site does not require an account and we do not collect personal data through forms. We currently run no analytics or advertising scripts and set no cookies. Your preferences (language, text size and eye-comfort mode) are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device and are never sent to us.

Third parties

  • Fonts: the site's fonts are served from our own pages, so no request goes to a font provider.
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log visits according to GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Product images: product photos are mostly served from this site itself (resized copies); if a copy is missing, they load from AliExpress's image servers, and AliExpress may then receive your IP address and browser information.
  • Store links: when you click a product link you leave our site for the store (AliExpress). The store may set cookies to credit a purchase to us, and its own privacy policy applies to you.

Affiliate disclosure

We take part in affiliate programs and may earn a commission on qualifying purchases made through our links at no extra cost to you.

Children

The site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy and will show the date of the latest update at the top. If we add analytics or advertising, we will update it to say so.
