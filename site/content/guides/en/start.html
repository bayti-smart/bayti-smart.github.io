You do not need to buy everything at once. A good first smart home is three or four devices that solve one real problem, run from one app, so you can see whether you actually use them.

Pick one app first

Many low-cost devices work through the Tuya / Smart Life app, others use their own app, and some use Zigbee with a gateway. Mixing many apps is the most common reason a smart home feels annoying. Choose one app, and one voice assistant such as Alexa or Google Home if you want voice control, then check compatibility on each product page.

A sensible first set

  • A smart plug or bulb: the easiest way to learn schedules and scenes.
  • A door/window or motion sensor: lets something happen automatically, such as a light turning on.
  • A camera: only if you have a clear place and reason for it.
  • A Zigbee gateway: only when you plan more than a few sensors.

Questions people ask

How much should I spend first?

Start small with one or two low-cost devices. Prices on this site are approximate and change, so check the store page before ordering.

Do I need to program anything?

No. Most devices are set up from an app. Programming only matters for advanced projects such as Arduino or Home Assistant.

Is it safe to order from an overseas store?

Check the seller's rating, the plug type and voltage, and the return terms. Our guides on plugs and electrical work cover the safety points.
